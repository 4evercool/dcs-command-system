## Why you exist

`dcs/hooks/dcs_gate.py` is not the only reader of the approval-marker contract.
Three more places compute a **raw sha256** of `IAP.md` and compare it to
`IAP-APPROVED`'s first line:

- `dcs/workflows/execute.md:35` — its step 2 verification
- `dcs/workflows/status.md:75`
- `dcs/references/forms.md:16` — documents the contract as "sha256 of IAP.md"

Once S2 lands, **these become stricter than the gate.** Against a CRLF-drifted
`IAP.md` they instruct a hard stop — *"someone edited IAP.md after approval"* —
and halt `/dcs-execute`, while the gate itself allows the edit. That is a live
false-halt in the same defect class.

The IC ruled this **in scope rather than a follow-up**, on the grounds that
shipping a changed contract with unmigrated readers is the canonical
non-shippable half-migration.

## Task

1. Replace the raw-sha one-liners at `dcs/workflows/execute.md:35` and
   `dcs/workflows/status.md:75` with a **membership check over the identical
   three-digest set S2 implements**:

   - `raw` — the file as-is
   - `lf = raw.replace(b'\r\n', b'\n')`
   - `crlf = lf.replace(b'\n', b'\r\n')` — **derived from `lf`, never from
     `raw`**
   - dedup; **fold only `\r\n`, leave a lone `\r` untouched**

   The stored hash matching **any** member means the marker is valid. Keep each
   workflow's surrounding instruction intact — you are changing *what is
   computed*, not what the workflow does about the answer.

2. Update `dcs/references/forms.md:16` to document the **digest-set** contract
   rather than "sha256 of IAP.md".

3. **Match S2's semantics exactly.** If you and S2 disagree about the rule, the
   gate is authoritative and the disagreement is a deviation — say so rather than
   guessing.
