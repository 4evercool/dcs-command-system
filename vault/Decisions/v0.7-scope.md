# Proposal: what earns the 0.7.0 label

**Proposed:** 2026-07-29, Fable (main session), at Owner request —
"assess what can become further functionality/effectiveness drastic
enough to bump the version to 0.7.0."
**Status:** ADOPTED — at the eleventh `/dcs-esg`, 2026-07-29, as drafted:
objective 5 pasted into `STRATEGY.md` (row ranks stripped from the pasted
text — a rank is a derived fact with a lifetime), rank 1 →
`prompt-vs-schema-drift` (M→H), the `bin/dcs.js` fold taken
(`version-bump-command` → `doctor-version-only-check`),
`halt-enumeration-grammar-drift` promoted as the telemetry enabler. The tenth session (2026-07-29) made the ranking half of
this call by putting `schemas-contract-format` at rank 1 — and that
incident **landed the same day this proposal was written** (merge
`5a9015a`, v0.6.14, absorbing `json-examples-unparsed` at its stem):
every `schemas.md` contract section now declares one producer and a
one-field-per-row table, every charter carries a matching
`<output_contract>` block, and checks 18 (one-way schema→charter field
guard) + 19 (every fenced JSON block parses) hold it. **That was the
on-disk half — the format and its arbiter. The 0.7.0 label belongs to
the off-disk half**, which the format exists to enable: validating
prompts at dispatch and returns at receipt (`prompt-vs-schema-drift`,
rank 6), the direction no guard over the tree can see. Shipping the
format as a 0.6.x patch was correct under this proposal's own
definition — a better on-disk guarantee is a refinement; runtime
validation of the seam is the new mechanism class.
**Adopt by:** pasting the objective text below into `STRATEGY.md`'s
Long-term objectives as objective 5, and recording the sequencing
decisions in the same session's notes.

## The definition this rests on

In this package's own history a minor bump is **a new mechanism class,
not an accumulation of refinements**: 0.5.0 was the doctrine diet (hot
path split), 0.6.0 was the intake hook (DCS offering itself instead of
waiting to be invoked). All thirteen 0.6.x releases refined existing
mechanisms. Regenerate the precedent: `grep -nE "^## 0\." CHANGELOG.md`.

## Sequencing the eleventh session should settle

1. **Headroom is still the standing constraint, but its shape changed.**
   v0.6.14 demonstrated pay-as-you-go: it funded its own tables by
   deleting a redundant paragraph, landing at the identical 37,882 B.
   `Backlog.md` item 26 (written by that incident's own close) states
   the discipline: plan the trim or ratchet act at 202 time. The
   standalone-trim-first sequencing this proposal originally argued is
   therefore softened to: any hot-path-touching incident budgets its
   own bytes, and `workflow-file-trim-grandfathered` remains queued for
   the workflow-line half (`plan.md` 666, `execute.md` 424).
2. **The remaining arc is `prompt-vs-schema-drift` then
   `check-14-hardening`** — the first is the 0.7.0 mechanism, the
   second shares `tests/test_doctrine_integrity.py` with nothing else
   in the arc anymore (v0.6.14 discharged the three-row collision the
   register warned about; only `check-14-hardening` still holds that
   file).
3. **The `bin/dcs.js` fold is already suggested by its own rows** —
   `doctor-version-only-check`'s intake note says "worth weighing
   whether to fold the two together." This proposal weighs it: yes,
   fold; a `doctor` that verifies content and a `bump` that edits two
   files in sync are one incident's worth of territory.
4. **Telemetry rides, never leads.** Fix the regex (rank 14), then the
   table is a `close.md` addendum — plausibly foldable into whichever
   incident next touches `close.md` under the priority bar.

## Explicitly out of 0.7

- **Register fenced structured blocks** — the named fallback in
  [[sqlite-migration-register]], gated behind its own reopen trigger
  (a measured retrieval-cost bottleneck or a recurrence of the
  format-breakage class). Pulling it forward without the trigger firing
  would relitigate a decision one day old.
- **An ESG verification seat** (adjacent to Backlog #25) — a real gap
  with exactly one project's worth of evidence; building it now is the
  speculative-mechanism trap `Meta/building-dcs-lessons` §5 names.

## Semver note

Contract blocks live inside the shipped package (`schemas.md`,
`agents/**`) and change no user-side data format, so 0.7.0 stays a
minor bump under 0.x conventions. The converter-plus-format-version
shape that made the register migration "arguably major" does not apply
here — unless the register fenced-block fallback is ever pulled in,
which is one more reason it stays out.
